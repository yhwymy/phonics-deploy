三、5 段小对话

1. 在学校

A: Good morning, Tom.

B: Good morning, Miss Li.

A: How are you today?

B: I am fine, thank you. And you?

A: I am fine, too. Let's read the book.

2. 在家里

A: Mum, I am hungry.

B: What do you want to eat?

A: I want some noodles.

B: OK. Here you are.

A: Thank you, Mum.

3. 谈喜欢的东西

A: What do you like, Sam?

B: I like dogs. What about you?

A: I like cats. They are cute.

B: Yes, they are.

4. 买东西

A: Good afternoon. Can I help you?

B: Yes. I want a pencil.

A: Here you are. One pencil.

B: Thank you. Goodbye!

5. 谈天气

A: It is sunny today. Let's go to the park.

B: Good idea! I like the park.

A: Look! A bird in the tree.

B: It is beautiful.

四、3 篇小短文

My Family

I have a happy family. My father is tall. My mother is kind. I have a little sister. She is five years old. We love each other. I love my family very much.

My School

My school is big. There are many classrooms. I like my teacher. She is nice. I have many friends at school. We play together every day. I love my school.

A Sunny Day

Today is sunny. I go to the park with my father. We see many flowers. They are red and yellow. A bird sings in the tree. I am happy today.

五、怎么用这些素材

- 词：每天挑 10 个读一读，认识就行。

- 句子：挑喜欢的几句，照着说一说。

- 对话：两个人轮流读，一个人读 A，一个人读 B。

- 短文：先听你读一遍，再让他自己读一遍，不用背。

这些素材先够用一阵子了，用完了、或者想换难一点的，你跟我说一声，我再给你新的。
